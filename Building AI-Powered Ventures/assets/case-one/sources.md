## Methodology alignment for slides 50–58

Define the scope and agree on acceptance checks before the first run. The incomplete report is explicitly a teaching example; a successful first run does not need a manufactured failure. Coaching improves the reusable procedure, records its source, owner and version, and retains the original evidence. Slide 56 labels its rule change as a previous-test example. Slide 57 compares against the same inputs and standards; slide 58 begins the next delegation with refreshed facts.
